Archistar AI PreCheck fast-tracks LA’s wildfire rebuild

Archistar has partnered with the City of Los Angeles and LA County to revolutionize the permitting process and accelerate rebuilding efforts in the wake of the devastating Los Angeles wildfires.

The project

Archistar joined forces with the City of Los Angeles and LA County to fast-track rebuilding after the devastating wildfires, deploying our AI-powered AI PreCheck technology to dramatically accelerate permit approvals and help residents return home faster.

Unveiled by Governor Gavin Newsom and reported on by the Los Angeles Times, this initiative brings world-first digital permitting technology to one of the country’s largest and most complex jurisdictions, cutting through backlogs by:

  • Automatically checking building designs for code compliance before submission
  • Helping property owners pre-validate plans, avoiding frustrating delays
  • Dramatically reducing permit review timelines for government agencies
  • Delivering faster, smarter, and fairer approvals for residents and businesses

 

As seen on

Now approved statewide across California

AI PreCheck is procured for statewide use, eliminating the need for local RFPs and making adoption fast and simple for any city, county, or agency in California.

If your agency is based in California and looking to reduce permitting backlogs and improve turnaround times, we’d love to show you how AI PreCheck can help. Get in touch for a demo.

Already, more than 30 forward-thinking municipalities across the US, Canada, and Australia are using AI PreCheck to streamline approvals, including Austin, TX; Surrey, BC; Cape Coral, FL; and Yonkers, NY. 

Join the growing network of cities embracing faster, smarter permitting.

What this means for developers & architects

If your city is participating in an AI PreCheck pilot, production, or partnership, you’ll be able to start pre-checking plans for compliance as soon as it’s live on your council’s website. Know someone at your local council who could benefit from AI PreCheck? We’d love an introduction. Get in touch and help bring AI PreCheck to your city.

While you’re waiting for AI PreCheck to go live in your area, explore the Archistar x Autodesk Forma integration. Unlock smarter planning, faster insights, and more sustainable design outcomes, all in one seamless workflow.
